Definitions

Framework reminders

DT capability

Individual-level computational representation retaining subject-specific state and able to predict or simulate clinically relevant state, trajectory, outcome, or response. Patient-level data alone are insufficient.

VCT capability

Identifiable treatment-versus-comparator trial or trial-like comparison in which model-generated or simulated patient-level evidence materially enters a principal comparison, calibration/replication analysis, or treatment-effect analysis.

DT-L2

Requires anchoring to a specific observed person. Direct patient-record input alone is insufficient.

TM0 / TM1 / TM2

Peer temporal lifecycle classes: no forward propagation; forward propagation without later assimilation; or later empirical observations revising the instantiated subject. They are not a maturity ranking.

Evidence provenance / Assumption scrutiny / Evaluation scope

These are distinct dimensions and are not combined into a quality score. “Not applicable” is an applicability state, not the lowest assumption scrutiny grade; scrutiny is ordered only among applicable claims.

Representation modality

Classified from the patient-derived object actually consumed by subject-specific instantiation or update, not merely from an upstream raw source modality.
